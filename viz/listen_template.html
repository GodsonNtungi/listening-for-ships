Hear the ships

Each window is 15 minutes of sea noise, played here as 15 seconds. The sound is rebuilt from the 25 pitch bands we are given, not the original recording.

Use headphones: most ship noise is a low rumble that laptop speakers drop. In each picture, time runs left to right, pitch runs bottom (32 Hz) to top (7.9 kHz), and brighter means louder.

Rebuilt from src/listen.py. Sea = a real South African recording. Natural ship = a ship already in that recording, which does not count. Added ship = a recorded vessel passage mixed in, which is what we must measure. "Our guess" is the splitter with the quiet-floor cap.

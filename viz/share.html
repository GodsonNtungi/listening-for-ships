Lesson 1. Share is about energy, not dB. Every 10 dB is 10 times the energy. So a ship 10 dB quieter than the sea is only 9%, and a ship 10 dB louder is 91%. Small dB changes near "equal" move the share a lot.

Lesson 2. A ship that doesn't count still makes the total bigger. It goes in the bottom of the fraction only. Add one as loud as the sea and the same added ship drops from 50% to 33%.

Lesson 3. The trap. With only a ship that doesn't count, the grid shows a clear ship, but the answer is 0%. This is where our model loses points: it sees "a ship" and guesses in between.
